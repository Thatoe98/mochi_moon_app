## Title (28)

Mochi Moon: Self-Love Ritual

## Short description

Daily affirmations, gratitude and mood check-ins with Mochi, your moon bunny.

## Full description

Mochi Moon is a soft, cozy self-love app with a 2-minute daily ritual. Check in with how you feel, read three illustrated affirmation cards, write one thing you are grateful for, and grow a gentle streak, with Mochi the moon bunny beside you.

Self-care that fits in two minutes. No pressure, no chat, no ads.

YOUR DAILY SELF-LOVE RITUAL
• Mood check-in: pick how you feel today and watch your mood calendar fill up
• Three affirmation cards, each with a short read-aloud moment so the words really land
• One gratitude line for your gratitude and wins jar
• A calm celebration and a streak that is kind when you miss a day

AFFIRMATION CARD DECK
• Swipe through beautifully illustrated positive affirmations and quotes
• Save the ones that feel like you in your personal journal
• Write your own affirmation cards
• Share a card with a friend who needs it today

SELF-LOVE TOOLS FOR EVERY DAY
• Gratitude and wins jar to keep your good moments
• Box breathing with Mochi when you need to slow down
• Tiny kindness quests, small things you can do for yourself or someone else
• Letters to your future self
• A hard-day kit with gentle ideas for days that feel heavy
• Mochi's room, with outfits and cozy items to collect

SELF-LOVE JOURNEYS (Premium)
• 7 and 14 day programs to build a softer relationship with yourself
• A weekly letter from Mochi, written from your week's rhythm

COSMIC YOU: A LITTLE ASTROLOGY ON THE SIDE
• Daily guidance for your zodiac sign, lucky colors and gentle things to avoid
• Moon phase and retrograde tracker
• Birth chart and love compatibility, using the Western tropical zodiac
• Home screen widget to keep a daily reminder close

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• What you write stays yours: your gratitude lines, letters, journal and own cards are never shared with AI or analytics
• Delete your account and your data from inside the app

Mochi Moon is for entertainment and self-reflection. It is not medical advice and not a substitute for professional help.

Download Mochi Moon and start your first self-love ritual tonight.
