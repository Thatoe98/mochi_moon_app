# Mochi Moon — first test version (0.1.0)

Thank you for trying Mochi Moon early! 🌙 This is a very first version, so
some things will be rough. Your honest feedback helps a lot.

## What it is

A soft, self-love app. Swipe through affirmation cards, read each one out
loud, and keep the ones that feel like you. There is also a gentle
astrology side (your sign, the moon, retrogrades, your birth chart and love
compatibility). It is for fun and self-reflection, never for predictions.

## Install (Android only)

1. Open the file `MochiMoon-dev-0.1.0+3.apk` on your phone.
2. Android will say it blocks installs from this source. Tap **Settings**,
   allow it for the app you opened the file from (Files, Chrome, Drive...),
   then go back and tap **Install**.
3. If Google Play Protect says the app is unrecognised, tap **More details**,
   then **Install anyway**. It is only unrecognised because it is not on the
   Play Store yet.
4. The app is called **Mochi Moon Dev**.

You need Android 7 or newer and an internet connection for sign-in and
daily readings.

## Please try

- **The deck:** wait for the ring to fill before a card unlocks. Swipe right
  to save, left to skip, up to share. Try Undo.
- **Your journal:** do the cards you saved show up?
- **Cosmic tab:** your sign for today, the Moon, retrogrades.
- **Birth chart and compatibility:** enter a birth date (time and place are
  optional) and add a partner.
- **Reminders and the home-screen widget.**
- **Language:** Me tab, then Language. The Burmese is machine-made and not
  yet checked, so tell me about anything that sounds odd.
- **Dark mode**, and a larger text size in your phone settings.
- **Airplane mode:** does the app still show something?

## What to tell me

- What confused you or felt slow.
- Anything that looked cut off, overlapped or hard to read.
- Anything that crashed or froze: what you were doing, and your phone model.
- What you liked and what you would change.
- Any text that felt unkind, scary or untrue.

A screenshot helps a lot. Please do not send screenshots that show other
people's private details.

## Good to know

- This is a test build, so your data lives in a test project. Please use
  nothing you would not want a friend to see.
- The app never asks for your microphone. Reading out loud is only a timer.
- To remove everything: Me tab, then **Delete my account**. It erases your
  account and data.
- Some screens are unfinished (for example Premium and the For You deck).
